`wikipediaGATN` turns public information into a reproducible global air-transportation network for modelling and network-science applications. I co-developed the package and its research workflow, with attention to data construction, validation, documentation, and reuse.

**Tools:** Python, NetworkX, data pipelines, automated testing, documentation, GitHub.

The package has been submitted to pyOpenSci for peer review.
