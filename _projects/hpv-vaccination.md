## The problem

HPV vaccination strategies must account for transmission dynamics, population heterogeneity, and long-term cervical-cancer prevention outcomes.

## My contribution

I contributed to the development and analysis of a mathematical model used to evaluate HPV vaccination and cervical-cancer prevention strategies in Alberta. This collaborative work connects mechanistic epidemiology with a concrete population-health decision problem.

**Methods and tools:** mathematical epidemiology, compartmental modelling, parameter estimation, sensitivity analysis, public-health modelling.

**Status:** Manuscript submitted to the _Canadian Journal of Public Health_.
